# Event card

Keep one event small enough to identify its actual cause. Record:

- Stable ID and proposal/confirmed status.
- Participants and what each knows at entry.
- Immediate wants, available resources, and who acts first.
- Trigger and causal actions in chronological order.
- Simpler alternatives and why someone rejects or cannot use them.
- What the player may do; prerequisites, observable result and failure behavior.
- Map/NPC/encounter changes; reversible and persistent results.
- Information visible to everyone versus optional evidence.
- Relationship change and its triggering act.
- Dialogue only after these facts exist.

Tragedy check: show the prior rule and choice causing loss; identify what preparation can change and what it cannot. Avoid new rules at the climax. State event-triggered deadlines clearly; do not silently add real-time NPC deaths.
